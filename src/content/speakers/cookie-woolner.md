Cookie Woolner is a cultural historian of race, gender, and sexuality in the modern U.S. She is an [Associate Professor in the History department at the University of Memphis](http://www.memphis.edu/history/faculty/faculty/cookie-woolner.php). Her current manuscript, *”The Famous Lady Lovers:” African American Women and Same-Sex Desire Before Stonewall* (under contract with UNC Press), explores the lives of of Black women who loved women in the Interwar era.
