Joshua B. Guild is an assistant professor who specializes in 20th-century African-American history, with a particular focus on urban communities and the making of the modern African diaspora. Professor Guild’s dissertation compared African-American and Caribbean migration and politics in the formation of two black communities in post-World War II New York and London. He is currently working on a book based on his dissertation.
