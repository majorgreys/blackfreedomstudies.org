Edward Onaci teaches courses on African American history, modern US history, women’s global political struggles, social movements and music in Africa and its diaspora, and more. They incorporate interdisciplinary scholarship and emphasize student empowerment. Dr. Onaci’s instruction also makes use of creative resources and high impact tools, such as Reacting to the Past and Digital Liberal Arts projects. His approach encourages students to take ownership over their personal and group studies, produce their own knowledge, and apply what they learn in class to their current lives and future ambitions. His book, [Free the Land: The Republic of New Afrika and the Pursuit of a Black Nation-State](https://uncpress.org/book/9781469656144/free-the-land/), was released in 2020.
