Joshua M. Myers is an Associate Professor of Africana Studies in the [Department of Afro-American Studies at Howard University. ](https://afroamericanstudies.howard.edu/)He is the author of [*Of Black Study* (Pluto, 2023)](https://www.plutobooks.com/9780745344126/of-black-study/), [*Cedric Robinson: The Time of the Black Radical Tradition* (Polity, 2021)](https://politybooks.com/bookdetail/?isbn=9781509537914), and [*We Are Worth Fighting For: A History of the Howard University Student Protest of 1989*](https://nyupress.org/9781479811755/we-are-worth-fighting-for/)(NYU Press, 2019), as well as the editor of [*A Gathering Together: Literary Journal.*](https://www.agatheringtogether.com/)

In addition to serving on the board of the Association for the Study of Classical African Civilizations and the editorial board of *The Compass: Journal of the Association for the Study of Classical African Civilizations*, he is the senior content producer at the Africa World Now Project, and served as the co-coordinator of the SNCC Legacy Project’s Black Power Chronicles Oral History Project and as an organizer with Washington DC’s Positive Black Folks in Action. He is currently working with SNCC Legacy Project's Digital Movement Platform.
