Jesse Hagopian is a high school teacher in Seattle and has taught for over a decade at Garfield High School–the site of the historic [boycott of the MAP test ](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l5U5Hvj-xLQ)in 2013. Jesse is an editor for the social justice periodical [*Rethinking Schools*](http://www.rethinkingschools.org/index.shtml), is the co-editor of the books, [*Black Lives Matter at School*](https://www.haymarketbooks.org/books/1607-black-lives-matter-at-school) and [*Teaching for Black Lives*](https://rethinkingschools.org/books/teaching-for-black-lives/), and is the editor of the book, [*More Than a Score: The New Uprising Against High-Stakes Testing.*](https://www.haymarketbooks.org/books/618-more-than-a-score) Jesse serves as the Director of the [Black Education Matters Student Activist Award](https://blackeducationmatters.org/), is an organizer with the [Black Lives Matter at School](https://blacklivesmatteratschool.com/) movement, and is founding member of [Social Equity Educators](http://socialequityeducators.org/) (SEE).

Jesse is an activist, public speaker, and a contributing author to [101 Changemakers: Rebels and Radicals Who Changed US History](https://www.haymarketbooks.org/books/401-101-changemakers), [Education and Capitalism: Struggles for Learning and Liberation](https://www.haymarketbooks.org/books/393-education-and-capitalism) (Haymarket Books), and [Why We Teach Now](https://www.tcpress.com/why-we-teach-now-9780807755877) (Teachers College Press). Jesse’s essay on the MAP test boycott and the ensuing national uprising against high-stakes testing was published in Howard Zinn and Anthony Arnove’s 10th anniversary edition of [*Voices of a People’s History of the United States*](https://www.sevenstories.com/books/3837-voices-of-a-people-s-history-of-the-united-states).*
*

Jesse’s commentary has been featured on many local and national news programs including, [HBO’s “Problem Areas” with Wyatt Cenac](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r0ziIRPcl4s), NBC’s “Education Nation,” and The [PBS News Hour with Gwen Ifill](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/show/common-core). Jesse’s writings on education, the Black Lives Matter movement, Haiti, Palestine, and US politics, have been published at the Black Agenda Report, *The Progressive*, Alternet, and the National Education Association’s *Education Votes* blog. 

Jesse is the recipient of the 2019 [“Racial Justice Teacher of the Year”](https://www.facebook.com/SeattlePublicSchools/videos/426609328133651/?__xts__%5B0%5D=68.ARC209ozCMCVZwnZRfnI9i-mDGADQfK7ZqF22YJBuV-A-9YpDx1jTZkM08AfCOMmorY_IclJkAous_PI5CYE2F5oZvgs_ywyGCp2M8Y886WhGgtSZJCLYGu-N_yzZy7FH06jrE96tOZr2fG9gb11IbEEiw0D1nu97zIeiZC4mi9BwXqCAOELPAXXSBRezOOeLBHIPbAJM79Os0oC0BJAGeERcuuR2dxxj9x_4PKKtDdLqkReueQJ61WVpqP9gL-f0lClnojnW4-RdPjpbqE3H9mpKeL5bx6TrhRPLwJWhlObrEqP7fD1-kzxQCp5Pjw1uQaKAOHiB5Kk7TMtkcQXUXWGx5jLWE6GoOfRw2OO&__tn__=-R) from the NAACP Youth Coalition and the [“Social Justice Teacher of the Year”](https://www.facebook.com/JesseHagopianForSEAPres/photos/a.761884267193838/2362275467154702/?type=3&theater) award from Seattle Public School’s Department of Racial Equity. In 2015, Jesse received the Seattle/King County NAACP Service Award, was named as an [Education Fellow](http://www.progressive.org/news/2015/09/188323/progressive-launches-new-project-defend-public-education-progressive-education) to *The Progressive*magazine, as well as a “[Cultural Freedom Fellow](http://www.lannan.org/cultural-freedom/detail/jesse-hagopian-awarded-2015-cultural-freedom-fellowship)” for the Lannan Foundation for his nationally recognized work in promoting critical thinking and opposing high-stakes testing.

Jesse is a graduate of Seattle’s Garfield High School and Macalester College, and obtained his Master’s degree in teaching at the University of Washington.
