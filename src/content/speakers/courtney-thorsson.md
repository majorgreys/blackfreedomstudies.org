Courtney Thorsson teaches, studies, and writes about African American literature at the University of Oregon, where she is a Professor of English and a Faculty Fellow in the Clark Honors College. She is the author of [*Women’s Work: Nationalism and Contemporary African American Women's Novels*](https://www.amazon.com/Womens-Work-Nationalism-Contemporary-Literatures-ebook/dp/B00BNGJZCY/ref=tmm_kin_swatch_0?_encoding=UTF8&qid=&sr=) and essays in *Callaloo*, *African American Review, MELUS, Gastronomica, Contemporary Literature, Legacy, *and* Public Books.*

Courtney’s most recent book, [*The Sisterhood: How A Network of Black Women Writers Changed American Culture*](https://www.amazon.com/Sisterhood-Network-Writers-Changed-American/dp/0231204728), tells the story of a community of Black women writers and intellectuals who transformed political, literary, and academic cultures. Read reviews of *The Sisterhood* in [*The Nation*](https://www.thenation.com/article/culture/the-sisterhood-courtney-thorsson/), the [*Times Literary Supplement*](https://www.the-tls.co.uk/articles/the-sisterhood-courtney-thorsson-sing-a-black-girls-song-imani-perry-book-review-harvey-young/), [*Publisher's Weekly*](https://www.publishersweekly.com/9780231204729), [*Kirkus Reviews*](https://www.kirkusreviews.com/book-reviews/courtney-thorsson/the-sisterhood-thorsson/), [*Library Journal*](https://www.libraryjournal.com/review/the-sisterhood-how-a-network-of-black-women-writers-changed-american-culture-2194444), [*Signs: Journal of Women in Culture and Society*](https://www-journals-uchicago-edu.uoregon.idm.oclc.org/doi/full/10.1086/733647), [*The Journal of American History*](https://academic.oup.com/jah/article/111/3/609/7921815?login=true), and [*Resources for Gender and Women's Studies: A Feminist Review*](https://cms.library.wisc.edu/gwslibrarian/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2025/01/RGWS_4534_Sum-Fall_2024_Pritchard_Sisterhood.pdf)

*The Sisterhood* was on the [Brooklyn Public Library Book Prize 2024 long list for nonfiction](https://www.bklynlibrary.org/support/bpl-book-prize), received Honorable Mention for the [2024 William Sanders Scarborough Prize](https://www.mla.org/Resources/Career/MLA-Grants-and-Awards/Winners-of-MLA-Prizes/Annual-Prize-and-Award-Winners/William-Sanders-Scarborough-Prize-Winners) from the Modern Language Association, was a [2025 Oregon Book Award finalist](https://literary-arts.org/2025/01/announcing-the-2025-oregon-book-awards-finalists-and-special-awards-recipients/), and is a Literary Freedom Project [One Book One Bronx](https://onebookonebronx.com/) selection for 2025.
