Dr. Kinchen is Associate Professor of Pan-African Studies at the University of Louisville. She holds a PhD from the Department of History and Geography
at the University of Central Oklahoma. She studies African American history, with a particular focus on the Civil Rights Movement and the Black Power Movement, and 20th Century US History.

Her book, *[Black Power in the Bluff City: African American Youth and Student Activism in Memphis, 1965-1975](https://utpress.org/title/black-power-in-the-bluff-city/) *and her continuing research examines the impact, influence, and intersection of the Civil Rights and Black Power Movements on youth and student activism in Memphis, Tennessee.
