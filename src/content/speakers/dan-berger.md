Dan Berger is a historian of activism, Black Power, and the carceral state. His latest book is Stayed on Freedom: The Long History of Black Power Through One Family's Journey, published by Basic Books. He is professor of comparative ethnic studies at the University of Washington Bothell and curator of the [Washington Prison History Project](https://waprisonhistory.org/), a digital archive of prisoner activism and policy. Follow him @dnbrgr or [www.danberger.info](https://danberger.info/)
