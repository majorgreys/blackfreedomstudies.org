Dr. Vanessa K. Valdés is a writer and independent scholar whose work focuses on the literatures, visual arts, and histories of Black peoples throughout the Western hemisphere. An engaging speaker, she served as a professor at the City University of New York for seventeen years, from 2007-2024, earning the rank of full professor. She is the editor of*[Let Spirit Speak! Cultural Journeys through the African Diaspora ](https://sunypress.edu/Books/L/Let-Spirit-Speak2)*(2012);*[The Future Is Now: A New Look at African Diaspora Studies ](https://www.cambridgescholars.com/product/978-1-4438-3638-8)*(2012);*[Racialized Visions: Haiti and the Hispanic Caribbean](https://sunypress.edu/Books/R/Racialized-Visions)*(2020); and, with Earl E. Fitz,*[Machado de Assis, Blackness, and the Americas](https://sunypress.edu/Books/M/Machado-de-Assis-Blackness-and-the-Americas)*(2024). She is the author of*[Oshun's Daughters: The Search for Womanhood of the Americas](https://sunypress.edu/Books/O/Oshun-s-Daughters)*(2014) and [*Diasporic Blackness: The Life and Times of Arturo Alfonso Schomburg*](https://sunypress.edu/Books/D/Diasporic-Blackness)(2017). From 2021-2023, she co-curated*[Juan de Pareja, Afro-Hispanic Painter](https://www.metmuseum.org/exhibitions/juan-de-pareja)*, an exhibition at The Metropolitan Museum of Art, and co-authored its exhibition catalogue,[*Juan de Pareja, Afro-HIspanic Painter in the Age of Velázquez*](https://yalebooks.yale.edu/book/9781588397560/juan-de-pareja/) (2023). She is the editor of the [Afro-Latinx Futures](https://sunypress.edu/Series/S/SUNY-series-Afro-Latinx-Futures) [series](https://sunypress.edu/Series#AAS) at the State University of New York Press and is the co-editor of the [Global Black Writers in Translation](https://www.vanderbiltuniversitypress.com/search-results-grid/?series=global-black-writers-in-translation) [series](https://www.vanderbiltuniversitypress.com/global-black-writers-in-translation/#:~:text=The%20Global%20Black%20Writers%20in,their%20source%20languages%20into%20English.) at Vanderbilt University Press.
