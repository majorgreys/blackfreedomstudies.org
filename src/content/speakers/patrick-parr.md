Patrick Parr is the author of three books, most recently “[Malcolm Before X](https://www.umasspress.com/9781625348166/malcolm-before-x/),” which Kirkus Reviews praised as being “the definitive story of Malcolm’s youth and early adulthood.” He is also the author of *One Week in America: The 1968 Notre Dame Literary Festival and a Changing Nation* and *The Seminarian: Martin Luther King Jr. Comes of Age.* Parr’s work has appeared in The American Prospect, Japan Today and The Atlantic, among others. He lives with his wife near Tokyo and teaches writing at Lakeland University Japan.
