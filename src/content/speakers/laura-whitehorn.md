Laura Whitehorn spent 14 years in federal prison for the "Resistance Conspiracy" case and was released in 1999. She edited "The War Before: The True Life Story of Becoming a Black Panther, Keeping the Faith in Prison, & Fighting for Those Left Behind," by the late Black Panther political prisoner Safiya Bukhari. She works with [Release Aging People In Prison/RAPP](http://rappcampaign.com/) in New York City.
