Join us on Thursday, December 3rd at 6:30 PM for an in-person event at the Schomburg Center focused on revolutionary music. Mary D. Williams, co-author of *Mahalia Jackson, Moving On Up a Little Higher: The Story of an American Civil Rights Pioneer*, will discuss the legendary "Queen of Gospel" who used her transcendent voice to become an overlooked but guiding force of the civil rights movement.  Quito Swan will present on *Born a Sufferah: Dancehall Music's Insurgent Soundscapes,* incisively reimagining the internationalist, diasporic, and decolonial politics of dancehall music. Jibola Fagbamiye, co-author with Conor McCreery of *Fela: Music is a Weapon,* will discuss the Afrobeats and political struggles in Nigeria. Soyica Diggs Colbert will present on *Freedom’s Gate: Maya Angelou, Lorraine Hansberry, Abbey Lincoln, Miriam Makeba, & Nina Simone,* illuminating the entangled lives of five foundational cultural workers and freedom fighters.
