What has been and what is the role of the church in the black struggle for justice, then and now?  How have key civil rights leaders like Anna Arnold Hedgeman and churches like Harlem's Abyssinian Baptist Church shaped the Black struggle for social justice and spiritual liberation? What is that history? How important is the church today in the struggle for human rights, including Voting Rights?
