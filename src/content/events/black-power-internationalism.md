Please join us on Thursday, October 1st at 6:30 PM for an exciting online discussion about Black power internationalism. Martha Biondi, author of *We Are Internationalists: Prexy Nesbitt and the Fight for African Liberation*, will discuss Nesbitt’s life in struggle in Chicago and beyond. Russell Rickford will present on *A Proxy Africa: Guyana, African Americans, and the Radical 1970s*, illuminating the struggles in an often overlooked site of Black Power militancy. Sam Klug, author of *The Internal Colony: Race and the American Politics of Global Decolonization*, will draw out the threads of solidarity between Black freedom struggles in the US and anti-colonial liberation movements abroad. Akinyele Umoja will discuss *The Memoirs of Robert and Mabel Williams: African American Freedom, Armed Resistance, and International Solidarity*, a recently published volume from two pivotal Black Power organizers whose lives connected the US Black Freedom struggle to China and Cuba.
